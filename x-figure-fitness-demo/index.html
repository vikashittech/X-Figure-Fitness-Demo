Frequently Asked Questions

GOT QUESTIONS? WE HAVE ANSWERS

Learn more about memberships, diet tracking, trainers, batch timings, and facilities at X Figure Fitness.

Why is X Figure Fitness considered the best gym in Mookkannoor & Kerala?
X Figure Fitness combines a spacious high-ceiling facility with proper natural ventilation so you never feel suffocated during heavy workouts. We provide modern equipment, weekly trainer diet check-ins, Saturday Cardio, and certified trainers (Sumesh, Kannan, Praveen, Anoop) with a 4.7★ rating across 153 Google reviews.
Do trainers provide weekly diet plans and progress checks?
Yes! All members receive personalized weekly diet planning and accountability check-ins from our trainer team to ensure continuous fat loss or muscle building progress.
What are the gym timings in Indian Standard Time (IST)?
We operate Monday through Saturday in two convenient batches: Morning Batch (5:30 AM – 11:30 AM IST) and Evening Batch (4:30 PM – 9:30 PM IST). Sundays are closed for muscle recovery.
Is the gym suitable for beginners and women?
Yes, X Figure Fitness is highly beginner-friendly and offers a safe, respectful environment for ladies. Our trainers provide step-by-step guidance on exercise form and machine usage.
What special sessions are offered during the week?
We host high-intensity Saturday Group Cardio sessions led by trainer Anoop as well as guided yoga and mobility classes for joint health and recovery.
Is vehicle parking available at the gym?
Yes, our location on Mookkannoor Ezhattumugham Road features ample parking space for cars, two-wheelers, and cycles in a peaceful, easy-to-access area.
